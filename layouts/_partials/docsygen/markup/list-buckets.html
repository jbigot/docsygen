    each, of lines (docsygen/markup/list-line.html), with a small gap between two; a bucket that starts a label follows that label, as a small paragraph: static, protected, private, package, related, a member group's heading, or "inherited from" its base, linked.     %s

    ` $out $label -}} {{- end -}} {{- $prev = .label -}} {{- $lines := "" -}} {{- range .entries -}}{{- $lines = printf "%s%s" $lines (partial "docsygen/markup/list-line.html" (dict "ctx" $ctx "entry" . "page" $page)) -}}{{- end -}} {{- $out = printf `%s
      %s
    ` $out $lines -}} {{- end -}} {{- return safeHTML $out -}}
